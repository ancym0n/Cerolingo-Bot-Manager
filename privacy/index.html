Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Overview of Data Practices

CerolingoBM is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no servers, no user accounts, and no analytics. It does not send any information to the developer, and nothing you enter in the app ever leaves your device except for the requests to Duolingo described below.

2. What Is Stored on Your Device

The app saves your settings (lessons per account, mode toggles, username prefix, and email prefix and domain) in the local storage of the app window. Accounts created by the app are saved to accounts.txt inside the app's python/logs folder. Each entry contains the email, the password, and the session cookies of that account, including its login token, and it is stored as plain text. The Manage Accounts button simply opens that file in your default text editor. Delete the file or uninstall the app to remove this data.

3. Network Connections

The app only talks to Duolingo (duolingo.com) to register accounts, send XP session requests with your saved tokens, and load Duolingo in a browser window for the auto solver. What Duolingo does with that traffic is covered by its own privacy policy. The app window also loads the Nunito font from Google Fonts, so Google can see your IP address when it opens.

If Selenium, Requests, or Playwright are missing, the Python scripts install them from PyPI, and Playwright may download a Chromium build. Links in the app open in your default browser. There is no telemetry, crash reporting, or automatic update check.

4. Browser Automation

Creating accounts launches Chrome through ChromeDriver, and the auto solver launches Chromium through Playwright. Both run locally with a fresh temporary profile, not your own, so your personal bookmarks, cookies, and history are not touched. Closing the window ends the session.

5. This Website

This site does not use cookies or analytics. Your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, and the page asks the GitHub API and shields.io for the latest release, download count, and star count, so those services can see your IP address. The last updated date is cached in your browser's session storage until you close the tab.

6. Core Compliance Declarations

CerolingoBM guarantees the following:

  • No Data Collection or Sale: Nothing from the app is collected, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • No Tracking: The app has no analytics, advertising, or background tracking.
  • You Stay in Control: All saved data is in files on your device, and the full source code is open on GitHub.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app changes how it handles data. Any updates will be published on this page.
