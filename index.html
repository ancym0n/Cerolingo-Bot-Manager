FAQ

1. What is your Privacy Policy?

CerolingoBM respects your privacy. The app runs on your computer, has no analytics, and does not send anything to the developer. Your settings and the accounts you create are saved in local files on your device and only used to talk to Duolingo.

View Privacy Policy

2. What is CerolingoBM?

CerolingoBM stands for Cerolingo Bot Manager. It is a desktop application built to automate creating Duolingo accounts and managing them in bulk, including completing lessons and farming XP.

3. What are its main features?

CerolingoBM includes an Account Creator with custom username and email prefixes, automatic Leaderboard Qualification, bulk XP Farming across saved accounts, built-in Loop and Console modes, and an Account Manager that stores credentials and session tokens locally.

4. How does it work?

The app pairs an Electron dashboard with Python automation scripts. Account creation uses Selenium and ChromeDriver to register accounts and grab session cookies, while XP farming sends direct session requests using saved tokens from accounts.txt.

5. Which operating systems are supported?

CerolingoBM is currently built for Windows since it relies on bundled .exe binaries and Windows utilities. Support for other operating systems may be added in future updates.
